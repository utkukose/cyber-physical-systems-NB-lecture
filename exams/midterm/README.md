# Midterm project: A model and a controller for a cyber-physical system

Students choose a small cyber-physical system, such as a heated room, a battery or a water tank. They model it, design and tune a controller and test it in simulation with the methods of Weeks 1 to 7.

**Timing and weight.** Due at the end of Week 7. The project counts 40 percent of the course grade. The full project page with its options, starter Colab notebooks and rubric is published before the project starts.
