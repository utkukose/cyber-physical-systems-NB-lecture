# Datasets

The course uses no external dataset. All data in the Colab notebooks of the weeks and in the starter Colab notebooks of the projects are simulated, with the assumptions stated next to the code, so that every result can be reproduced without a download. The interactive labs record the student's own experiments, for example the poll cycles measured on the Modbus bench in Week 8. They keep these data only in the browser until the student copies them into a Colab notebook. Students who use public datasets or logs of real systems in their projects state the source, the licence and the permission for each of them in the report.
