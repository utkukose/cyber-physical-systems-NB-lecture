# Development templates

Each week adds a development template: A small, working Python program that carries out one method of the week and can be reused in projects. The templates run with the Python standard library and NumPy unless their header states otherwise, and each starts with a description, its inputs and outputs, and an example run.

| Week | Template | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Week 1](../weeks/week-01/README.md) | [`power_balance_day.py`](power_balance_day.py) | Computes the hourly balance of a site from its demand, solar and wind models and a battery rule, with the energies, the peak import and the self-sufficiency. |
| [Week 1](../weeks/week-01/README.md) | [`sense_compute_actuate.py`](sense_compute_actuate.py) | A skeleton of a periodic control loop with a sensor, a controller and an actuator as small classes, run against a simulated plant. |
| [Week 2](../weeks/week-02/README.md) | [`building_preheat.py`](building_preheat.py) | Simulates a building with its RC model and a heating schedule, and finds the latest start of the heater for a target temperature by bisection. |
| [Week 2](../weeks/week-02/README.md) | [`first_order_simulation.py`](first_order_simulation.py) | Simulates any model dx/dt = f(t, x, u) with the methods of Euler, Heun and Runge-Kutta, and compares them with an exact solution. |
| [Week 3](../weeks/week-03/README.md) | [`overcurrent_coordination.py`](overcurrent_coordination.py) | Computes the trip times of the IEC curves for overcurrent relays and the time multiplier of an upstream relay for a grading margin. |
| [Week 3](../weeks/week-03/README.md) | [`state_machine_toolkit.py`](state_machine_toolkit.py) | Runs, tests and checks state machines given as tables: traces, reachable states, states without a way out, safety properties and the conformance of logs. |
| [Week 4](../weeks/week-04/README.md) | [`meter_pulses.py`](meter_pulses.py) | Turns the times of the pulses of a meter into power, integrates a power profile into intervals of 15 minutes and reports the demands. |
| [Week 4](../weeks/week-04/README.md) | [`signal_conditioning.py`](signal_conditioning.py) | Calibrates a sensor at two points, checks a sampling rate for aliasing, quantises a signal and filters its noise with a moving average or an exponential filter. |
| [Week 5](../weeks/week-05/README.md) | [`loop_tuning.py`](loop_tuning.py) | Metrics of a step response, the stability limit of the integral gain by the Routh-Hurwitz criterion and the settings of Ziegler and Nichols. |
| [Week 5](../weeks/week-05/README.md) | [`pid_controller.py`](pid_controller.py) | A discrete PID controller with output limits, an anti-windup and a filtered derivative, written as a closure, and a test on the island of the campus microgrid. |
| [Week 6](../weeks/week-06/README.md) | [`kalman_filter.py`](kalman_filter.py) | A Kalman filter for any linear model, written as a class, with an example that tracks the state of charge of a battery and the offset of its current sensor. |
| [Week 6](../weeks/week-06/README.md) | [`wls_state_estimation.py`](wls_state_estimation.py) | State estimation by weighted least squares with the chi-square test and the largest normalized residual, removing bad data one measurement at a time. |
| [Week 7](../weeks/week-07/README.md) | [`plc_scan.py`](plc_scan.py) | The scan cycle of a programmable logic controller (PLC): the best and the worst reaction from an input terminal to an output terminal, the reactions to events at random moments and the watchdog. |
| [Week 7](../weeks/week-07/README.md) | [`rt_scheduler.py`](rt_scheduler.py) | Periodic real-time tasks as a class, the utilization tests of rate-monotonic (RM) and earliest-deadline-first (EDF) scheduling, the response-time analysis and a simulator of both schedulers. |
| [Week 8](../weeks/week-08/README.md) | [`modbus_rtu_frames.py`](modbus_rtu_frames.py) | Builds and parses Modbus RTU frames with their CRC, decodes registers into integers and floats and computes the poll cycle of a bus. |
| [Week 8](../weeks/week-08/README.md) | [`mqtt_topics_and_bridge.py`](mqtt_topics_and_bridge.py) | Matches MQTT topic filters, publishes on change with a deadband and a heartbeat, and bridges Modbus values to MQTT when the client library paho-mqtt is installed. |
| [Week 8](../weeks/week-08/README.md) | [`networked_loop_sim.py`](networked_loop_sim.py) | Simulates a first-order control loop with network delay, sampling and packet loss, and computes its delay margin. |
| [Week 9](../weeks/week-09/README.md) | [`battery_schedule_lp.py`](battery_schedule_lp.py) | The daily schedule of a battery and of flexible loads as a linear program with SciPy: the cheapest charging and discharging for hourly prices, an export price, a peak charge and loads that must receive their energy within a window. |
| [Week 9](../weeks/week-09/README.md) | [`economic_dispatch.py`](economic_dispatch.py) | The economic dispatch of generating units with quadratic costs and limits, by bisection on the incremental cost, with the price of each hour that follows from it. |
| [Week 10](../weeks/week-10/README.md) | [`load_forecast_regression.py`](load_forecast_regression.py) | A day-ahead forecast of an hourly load by linear regression on the hour, the type of day and the heating degrees, with the error measures MAE, RMSE and MAPE and two naive forecasts for comparison. |
| [Week 10](../weeks/week-10/README.md) | [`residual_cusum_detector.py`](residual_cusum_detector.py) | Anomaly detection on the residual of a model: a threshold on the standardised residual and a two-sided cumulative sum (CUSUM) that restarts after each alarm, scored against a log of known faults. |
| [Week 11](../weeks/week-11/README.md) | [`authenticated_frames.py`](authenticated_frames.py) | Authenticated Modbus frames: a counter and an HMAC tag with SHA-256 in front of the CRC, a receiver that rejects damaged, forged and replayed frames, and the cost of the longer frames in the poll cycle of a serial bus. |
| [Week 11](../weeks/week-11/README.md) | [`fdi_attack_check.py`](fdi_attack_check.py) | False data injection against a weighted least-squares state estimate: the chi-square test, the hidden injection a = H c that the test cannot see, and the rank test that tells which protected meters leave no hidden attack. |

Run a template with `python <file>` in a terminal or paste it into a Colab cell. The templates are licensed under the MIT License of the repository.
