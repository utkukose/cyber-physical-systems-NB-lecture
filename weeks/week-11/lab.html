Attack and defence lab

Two parts of this interactive lab are virtual laboratories in three dimensions (3D) with the hardware in the foreground. Part A attacks the state estimate from the main switchboard of the campus. Part C replays the frequency to the battery controller of the island. Part B computes the formulas of the lecture step by step, and Part D asks for the values of stage 11 by hand. In the calculator every intermediate value is rounded, and the next step continues with the rounded value. The results of Parts A and C can be copied into the Colab notebook. Each laboratory first shows its site. Start moves the view closer, and the view returns to the site at the end. The laboratories work best with a mouse or a touchpad on a computer.

Part A: The main switchboard

The main switchboard of the campus feeds the five buildings and the car park. Its feeder meters, the meters in the buildings and the main meter report to the control room. The control room estimates the six feeders by weighted least squares and tests the residuals with the chi-square test. When the estimate of the dormitory B3 exceeds 150 kW, the control room sheds B3. An attacker has plugged a laptop into the network switch and can change readings on their way.

Set the changes of the attacker in the table below the view, tick the meters whose readings carry a tag, and press Send the readings. Your missions: First, change one meter and see whether the control room notices. Second, make the control room shed B3 without any alarm. Third, tag six meters so that no hidden attack remains, and send your attack of the second mission again.

Part B: The estimate and the attacks, step by step

The calculator follows the formulas of the lecture for two feeders, their meters z1 and z2 and the main meter z3, all with σ = 2 kW. It computes the mismatch, the estimates and the test value. It then follows an attack on z1 alone, a hidden attack on z1 and z3 and a hidden attack with the main meter protected.

Part C: The island controller

In island mode the battery inverter holds the frequency of the campus with its droop. The battery controller in the control cabinet reads the frequency meter every 0.1 s over a short serial line. An attacker has cut into that line and inserted a small box. It records the frequency from 20 to 60 s and then plays the recording back to the controller. At 80 s a forged command starts three cars at the chargers with 66 kW.

Choose the watermark of the battery and the window of the detector below the view and press Run the attack. The detector alarms when the response g to the watermark falls below 0.5, and the battery then switches to its own measurement of the frequency. Your missions: First, run the attack without a watermark. Second, detect the replay before the cars start, without a false alarm. Third, do the same with a jitter of the frequency below 8 mHz.

Part D: By hand: Stage 11 of the microgrid

Two feeders and the main meter read $z_1 = 118$, $z_2 = 84$ and $z_3 = 212$ kW, all with $\sigma = 2$ kW: Compute the test value $J$. The attacker then adds 30 kW to $z_1$ and to $z_3$: Compute the estimate of feeder 1. Three steps of the island have the watermark $\omega = 10, -10$ and 5 kW and the movements $m = 2.0, 3.5$ and $-4.4$ mHz, with $\beta = 1.24$ mHz per kW: Compute the response $g$.
